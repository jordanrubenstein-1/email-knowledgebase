To our brand community,

We’re excited to share some important news: The Expert has been acquired by Havenly Brands.

Since launching e-commerce in 2023 with ~50 vendors, The Expert marketplace has been met with tremendous enthusiasm from both the trade and consumers. Over the years, we’ve grown to more than 200+ brands while maintaining a commitment to quality and curation. Simultaneously, we’ve built a robust trade program that rethinks how design firms operate and shop. We’re incredibly proud of what we’ve accomplished as an independent company.

We’re equally excited to be joining Havenly Brands—a portfolio of design-driven companies that brings meaningful scale, infrastructure, and technology to support and accelerate our growth, while staying true to what makes The Expert special.

We know this news may raise questions. We’ve shared a brief FAQ below, and if you have additional questions or would like to connect 1:1, we’d be happy to hop on a call.

Will The Expert continue to be a standalone experience?

Yes. This acquisition allows The Expert to access shared resources while continuing to operate as its own distinct brand and website. Our Expert and vendor communities will continue to serve the higher end of the market, offering best-in-class design expertise, products, and experiences.

Will my brand be listed on other Havenly Brands websites?

No. Your brand will continue to only be listed on The Expert.

How will this acquisition impact my business?

In the immediate, there will be no operational changes. In the future, this acquisition brings about greater access to resources and technology which will be leveraged to make working with The Expert more seamless—from onboarding and catalog management improvements to increasing your visibility and customers via a significantly increased audience.

Will pricing, margins, or vendor terms change?

There will be no immediate changes to pricing, margins, or vendor terms. Any future updates would be communicated clearly and well in advance.

Tell me more about Havenly Brands.

Havenly Brands is a leading portfolio of home and design brands serving both consumers and the trade across a wide range of styles, needs, and price points. Its portfolio includes Havenly, Interior Define, The Citizenry, The Inside, St. Frank, Burrow, and now The Expert—bringing together creativity, technology, and commerce to support clients and designers at every stage of design and business.
