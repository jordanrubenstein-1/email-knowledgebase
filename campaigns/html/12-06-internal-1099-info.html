*Please note this information only applies to US Experts* In the meantime, we wanted to send a quick note about end-of-year 1099 forms. 

 

Please make sure all of your tax settings are correct in Stripe. This includes: Your name, address, and Taxpayer Identification Number (Social Security Number /Employer Identification Number). You can access your stripe account directly through your Expert dashboard. Here is an article that should help: https://support.stripe.com/express/questions/how-do-i-update-my-tax-information 

 

This year, we’ve enabled e-delivery for the 2022 tax year so you will be able to receive your 1099 tax forms electronically rather than worrying about the mail. This is optional, but should make things easier.

 

You should have received an email from Stripe with instructions on how to set up e-delivery. By January 31, 2023: Your 1099 tax form will be available to download through Stripe. Your 1099 tax form will be mailed to you if you don’t receive an email from Stripe or don’t consent to e-delivery. Please allow up to 10 business days for postal delivery. The Expert will file your 1099 tax form with the IRS and relevant state tax authorities.
