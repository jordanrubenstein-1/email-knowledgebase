The fabric your sofa or chair is upholstered in can be make or break. No matter what upholstery option you choose, know that it has been designed and chosen to effortlessly elevate your every day, while being tough enough to resist those every day accidents.

Performance fabric

Our performance fabrics resists stains and scratches, with an abrasion rating over 30,000 double rubs (a metric of abrasion tolerance) and are Standard 100 Oeko-Tex Certified (tested for 400+ harmful substance).

Performance velvet

Chic and glamorous, but not at the expense of durability. In fact, liquids bounce off our velvet without any chemical coatings, and it’s rated at over 100,000 double-rubs (a metric of abrasion tolerance) and Standard 100 Oeko-Tex Certified (tested for 400+ harmful substance).

Top-grain leather

A beautiful, natural leather imported from an Italian tannery with more than a century of experience, using a state-of-the-art water filtration system that eliminates waste and is LWG certified.

Performance bouclé

An ultra comfortable woven fabric made of looped yarn. In many cases, it’s made from wool, which makes it extremely delicate. But our Performance Bouclé is made from resilient, recycled polyester, to resist stains and is Standard 100 Oeko-Tex Certified (tested for 400+ harmful substance).
