## Recommendations

Based on the send time analysis:

- **Prioritize sends between 7pm and 6pm local** — These hours consistently show higher engagement
- **Focus on Thursday and Wednesday** — Best performing days across all brands
- **Evening (18-21) sends outperform** — Consider shifting more volume to this window
- **Brand-specific timing matters** — Each brand has different optimal windows; use the brand matrix above for guidance

---

### Methodology Note

*Send times are inferred from campaign timestamps. Campaigns are classified by the span between first and last send: **Local-time sends** (span ≥ 20 hours) use the last_sent timestamp minus 10 hours (Hawaii offset) to recover intended local time. **Fixed-time sends** (span < 20 hours) convert last_sent directly to Mountain Time.*
