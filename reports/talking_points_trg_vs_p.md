# Key Talking Points: TRG_ vs P_ SMS Campaign Performance (Q4 2025)

## Executive Summary
- **Triggered (TRG_) campaigns** demonstrate significantly higher efficiency and engagement rates
- **Batch & Blast (P_) campaigns** drive higher total revenue through volume
- Clear opportunity to optimize campaign mix based on performance data

---

## 1. Engagement & Click-Through Performance

### TRG_ Campaigns: 6.3x Higher CTR
- **TRG_ Average CTR: 8.42%** vs **P_ Average CTR: 1.33%**
- Triggered campaigns are **6.3x more effective** at driving clicks
- **Key Insight**: Personalized, contextually-relevant messages (cart abandon, welcome series) resonate much better with recipients

### Talking Point:
> "Triggered SMS campaigns achieve an 8.42% click-through rate—over 6 times higher than batch campaigns. This demonstrates the power of sending the right message at the right time to the right person."

---

## 2. Revenue Efficiency: The Stunning Difference

### TRG_ Revenue per Million Sends: $13.5M vs P_ $72,591
- TRG_ campaigns generate **186x more revenue per million sends** than batch campaigns
- **TRG_ Revenue per Send: $13.50** vs **P_ Revenue per Send: $0.07**
- TRG_ is **186x more efficient** on a per-send basis

### Talking Point:
> "While batch campaigns drive more total revenue due to volume, triggered campaigns are dramatically more efficient—generating $13.5 million in revenue per million sends compared to just $72,591 for batch campaigns. That's a 186x efficiency advantage."

---

## 3. Volume vs. Efficiency Trade-off

### Total Revenue Comparison
- **P_ Total Revenue: $184,261** (124 campaigns, 2.5M sends)
- **TRG_ Total Revenue: $33,522** (28 campaigns, 2,483 sends)
- P_ campaigns drive **5.5x more total revenue** due to scale

### Talking Point:
> "There's a clear volume vs. efficiency trade-off. Batch campaigns sent 1,000x more messages and generated 5.5x more total revenue, but triggered campaigns are 186x more efficient per send. The question is: can we increase triggered campaign volume while maintaining their efficiency?"

---

## 4. Average Revenue per Campaign

### Similar Per-Campaign Performance
- **TRG_ Average: $1,197 per campaign**
- **P_ Average: $1,486 per campaign**
- P_ campaigns average **24% more revenue per campaign**, but this is offset by their much lower efficiency

### Talking Point:
> "Interestingly, average revenue per campaign is similar between the two types ($1,197 vs $1,486), but triggered campaigns achieve this with far fewer sends and much higher engagement rates."

---

## 5. Strategic Recommendations

### Key Takeaways:
1. **Expand Triggered Campaign Volume**: With 186x efficiency advantage, increasing TRG_ send volume could significantly boost revenue
2. **Optimize Batch Campaign Targeting**: 1.33% CTR suggests opportunities to improve segmentation and personalization
3. **Hybrid Approach**: Use batch campaigns for broad reach, triggered campaigns for high-value moments
4. **Test & Learn**: The efficiency gap suggests we should test more triggered campaign types and touchpoints

### Talking Point:
> "The data suggests a clear opportunity: if we can scale triggered campaigns from 2,483 sends to even 10% of batch volume (250K sends) while maintaining efficiency, we could generate over $3 million in additional revenue. The key is identifying more high-value trigger moments."

---

## 6. Campaign Mix Analysis

### Current State:
- **124 P_ campaigns** (batch/blast) - 2.5M sends
- **28 TRG_ campaigns** (triggered) - 2,483 sends
- **Ratio: 1,000:1** send volume (P_ to TRG_)

### Opportunity:
- If TRG_ campaigns maintained efficiency at 10x current volume (24,830 sends), estimated revenue: **$335,220**
- This would still be only 1% of batch volume but could drive significant incremental revenue

### Talking Point:
> "Currently, batch campaigns represent 99.9% of our SMS send volume. While they drive total revenue, triggered campaigns represent a massive untapped opportunity. Even a modest increase in triggered campaign volume could meaningfully impact revenue."

---

## 7. Bottom Line

### The Efficiency Paradox:
- **Batch campaigns**: Lower efficiency, higher total revenue (volume play)
- **Triggered campaigns**: Higher efficiency, lower total revenue (quality play)
- **Strategic question**: How do we scale quality without losing efficiency?

### Final Talking Point:
> "The data tells a clear story: triggered SMS campaigns are dramatically more efficient and engaging, but we're only scratching the surface of their potential. The strategic opportunity is to systematically identify and scale high-value trigger moments while maintaining the efficiency advantage that makes them so powerful."
