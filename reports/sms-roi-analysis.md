## Methodology & Caveats

- **Attribution model**: GA4 last-click. Sessions where `SESSIONPRIMARYCHANNELGROUP = 'SMS'` are credited to SMS.
- **Last-click bias**: Triggered flows (browse/cart abandonment) may understate SMS influence — the user may have clicked SMS but their last session before purchase came from another channel.
- **Send volumes**: Only available for BUR and CZ via the Braze Raw Events datashare. STF and ID send volumes are not in the datashare; ROAS cannot be computed for those brands.
- **Cost assumption**: $0.01/message is an estimate. Actual blended costs (carrier fees + Attentive/Braze platform) vary.
- **Excluded brands**: HAV (no SMS program), TI and TE (Klaviyo — no GA4 data pipeline).
- **Date range**: Trailing 365 days ending today.
