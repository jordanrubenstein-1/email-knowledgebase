# ID Trade Email Performance Report
**Interior Define Trade Program — Email Channel**
*Knowledgebase + GA4 data · Jul 2024 – May 2026 · Generated May 26, 2026*

---

## TL;DR

Trade emails are a small but high-value channel. They represent **3% of ID email sessions** but generate **$22 revenue per session vs $16 for consumer** — a 41% efficiency advantage. The welcome series alone has driven ~$45K in revenue and averages **64.5% open rates**. The list has grown from ~3K to ~22K over the past year, and engagement has stabilized at a healthy 43–47% open rate. July 2025 and May 2026 are the two standout revenue months, both driven by private/exclusive sale events.

## 4. What's Working

### ✅ Welcome / Triggered Series
The best single asset in the program. 5 canvas steps averaging **64.5% OR and 4.3% CR** — and $44,852 in attributed revenue (18 purchases, #3 all time). Welcome T1 alone: **72.3% OR / 12.6% CR**. Ensure this series is always on and fully optimized.

### ✅ Private / Exclusive Sale Events
July 2025 Private Sale was the single best revenue month ($97K). The top-performing subject line: *"You're Invited: Private Trade Sale — 25% Off Just for Designers"* → 56.5% OR. Exclusivity framing resonates strongly with trade professionals.

### ✅ Plain-Text Emails for Conversions
PT clicks are nearly 2× designed (1.61% vs 0.87%). Some of the highest-revenue campaigns are PTs. Trade customers respond to clear, direct, text-forward communication — PT is not a downgrade for this audience.

### ✅ Product-Pull Content (Swatch / In-Stock)
Only 3 swatch/in-stock emails in the dataset but they average **3.1% CR** — well above the 1.21% overall average. Trade customers are actively sourcing and click to request samples. More of this would likely perform.

### ✅ Cross-Brand Send Efficiency
Cross-brand trade emails (featuring ID + CZ + TI + STF) have similar performance to ID-only sends (44.2% OR vs 42.3%) at similar list sizes. Good reach efficiency with no material engagement penalty.

## 5. What to Watch / Fix

### ⚠️ All-Caps and Vague Promo Subject Lines Underperform
The weakest subject lines share a pattern — generic urgency, all-caps, or hype-first framing:

| Subject | OR |
|---|---|
| *"Introducing Contract Grade by Interior Define"* | 11.8% |
| *"You're Invited: SALE EARLY ACCESS"* | 15.8% |
| *"Just dropped: trade-exclusive discounts!"* | 16.8% |
| *"HAPPENING NOW: 25% OFF"* | 26.3% |

Trade professionals expect substantive, direct communication. Specificity and exclusivity (private, first, just for designers) work; hype and caps don't.

### ⚠️ April 2025 and March 2026 Were Near-Zero Revenue Months
- April 2025: 587 sessions, only **1 purchase / $2,304**
- March 2026: 660 sessions, only **1 purchase / $1,649**

High engagement, low conversion — these months likely had sparse offer calendars or drove traffic to pages that weren't converting. Worth a retrospective look at what ran those months.

### ⚠️ Q4 2025 Click Rate Drop (0.50%)
After recovering strongly in Q3 2025, clicks fell to 0.50% in Q4 — the lowest since the list scaled. 2026 Q2 is recovering (1.50%). Check whether Q4 2025 leaned too heavily on reminder/sale content without enough product or editorial variety.

### ⚠️ Active A/B Tests Awaiting Results
8 campaigns with no analytics yet from the ongoing Lapsed Re-Activation and Cross-Brand Version A/B tests (sent 5/5 and 5/12). Follow up once data is in — results will inform list health strategy.

## 6. Recommendations

| Priority | Action |
|---|---|
| 🔴 High | **Protect and optimize the Welcome series** — it's #3 in all-time revenue. Audit it quarterly, keep it fully on. |
| 🔴 High | **Run more private/exclusive sale events** — July 2025 was $97K. This format ($6,499 AOV!) is not one-off; replicate with holiday and fall sale. |
| 🟡 Medium | **Increase swatch / in-stock sends** — only 3 in 2 years, all strong. Trade customers are sourcing. A monthly cadence of what's available ships quickly would drive consistent clicks and conversion. |
| 🟡 Medium | **Default to PT for sale sends** — near-identical ORs, nearly 2× the click rate. Save design budget for editorial and product launch moments. |
| 🟡 Medium | **Fix subject line patterns for promo sends** — stop using all-caps and generic "invited / just dropped" framing. Use specificity: *"35% — First Time Ever for Trade"* outperforms *"HAPPENING NOW: 25% OFF"* by 30+ OR points. |
| 🟢 Low | **Investigate the April 2025 / March 2026 conversion gaps** — sessions were there, purchases were not. Landing page or offer mismatch. |
