## Methodology & Limitations

### What This Analysis Can Tell You
- **Correlations** between tactics and performance
- **Patterns** that warrant A/B testing
- **Baseline benchmarks** for comparison
- **Audience differences** between brands

### What This Analysis Cannot Tell You
- **Causation** — Correlation is not cause. A tactic may work because it's rare, not inherently better.
- **Novelty effects** — Text-only emails may outperform because they're uncommon. If everyone switched, the advantage would disappear.
- **Sequence effects** — A promo after 5 content emails performs differently than after 5 other promos.
- **Selection bias** — Certain tactics may only be used in specific contexts that inherently perform better.

### Recommended Approach
1. **Use findings as hypotheses** — Test before assuming causation
2. **Consider frequency** — A winning tactic used sparingly beats one used constantly
3. **Monitor for decay** — What works today may stop working as competitors adopt it
4. **Segment analysis** — These are averages; high-value segments may behave differently
